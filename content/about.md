## About me / What is with the handle?
Back in the day I played lineage 2 with some friends. We all created characters with `oth` at the end so we knew, roughly, who not to merc (mistakes were made). Smash comes from the fact that in most rpg games that I played I like a big ol' warhammer. So if you ever see a `smashoth` running around some rpg it could be me. Or... some evil impostor.  Feel free to msg some arcane ARM question. You will either get a answer, or come off as just another nutter online, lol.

I then started getting into comp sec about 2013 playing CTFs and so on. I then became aware of Charlie Miller through his awesome car hacking, just blew me away. And so, when I started needing a handle more often I settled on `0xSmash0th` inspired by Charlie's `0xcharlie` handle.
