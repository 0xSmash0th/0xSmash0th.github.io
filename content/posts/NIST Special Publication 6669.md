## Abstract

This Special Publication (SP 6669) establishes the first comprehensive national reference for quantifying fecal deposits in terms of mass, volume, and comedic magnitude. It defines the **Standard Poop Unit (SPU)**, presents rigorously *un*rigorous techniques for measuring fractional poops, and offers calibration guidance for bathroom scales that have witnessed unspeakable horrors.

## 1. Introduction

For centuries humanity has gazed into the porcelain abyss and muttered, "How much *was* that?!" Responding to this noble curiosity, we present a metrological framework nobody asked for but everybody deserves. This document harmonizes bathroom reality with NIST's tradition of sober, peer-reviewed precision (and occasional gag reflex).

## 2. Definitions

| Term | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| **Standard Poop Unit (SPU)** | Exactly **128 g ± 3 g**: the mass of a single healthy adult bowel movement produced after a 24-hour diet of hospital oatmeal and black coffee. |
| **Fractional Poop (fSPU)** | Any deposit smaller than 1 SPU. Reported in **millipoops (mSPU, 10⁻³ SPU)** or **micropoops (µSPU)**. Colloquially, "nugget." |
| **Multi-Flush Condition (MFC)** | An event requiring more than one flush. Must be logged separately, with a witness statement and a plumber's quote. |
| **Splash Factor (SF)** | Ratio of outward splash radius (cm) to water-line diameter (cm). SF > 1 warrants hazmat protocols. |

## 3. Measurement Methodology

### 3.1 Mass Determination

1. Place a laboratory balance (or your roommate's kitchen scale) beside the target receptacle.
2. Post-event, transfer the specimen using tongs and introspection.
3. Record mass in grams. Subtract the tare of any cling-ons.

*(Tongs should be stainless steel; mental scars may be permanent.)*

### 3.2 Volume Determination (Archimedes, but worse)

1. Fill a graduated cylinder with a known volume of water.
2. Gently submerge the specimen; record the displacement.
3. If it floats, either (a) see Section 4.2 on buoyancy-corrected density, or (b) call a gastroenterologist.

### 3.3 Consistency Grading (Bristol Alignment)

Compare the sample with the Bristol Stool Form Scale:

- Types 1–2 → fragments; adjust mass for crumble loss.
- Types 5–7 → splash factor may exceed 1; wear PPE.

The combined standard uncertainty, *u_c*, for a domestic measurement is **2 g (mass)** and **1 mL (volume)** with coverage factor *k = 2*, provided the operator keeps a straight face.

## 5. Example Calculation

A sample reads **64 g** on the balance and displaces **55 mL** of water.

```
SPU     = 64 g ÷ 128 g/SPU  = 0.50 SPU
density = 64 g ÷ 55 mL      = 1.16 g/mL
```

The event is therefore classified as a **Singular Nugget** (½-Poop), comfortably within the *We-Can-Still-Flush* regime.
